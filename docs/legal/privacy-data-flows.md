# Privacy notice inputs and data flows — unpublished draft

Operator identity/contact, customer controller roles, applicable jurisdictions, lawful bases and user-rights contacts: **pending legal/operator review**. Do not substitute the product name for a legal entity.

Accounts store profile, verified email, password hashes, federation identifiers, MFA envelopes, session/device metadata, organization and workspace memberships. Workspace services store conversations/messages, tasks/projects/comments, files and versions, governed knowledge/CRDT/history, provenance and agent configuration. Retained media/transcripts require the selected call mode and consent; transient caption handling and separately enabled voice-agent processing must be described per actual provider.

Execution receives only currently authorized source context and dispatches to the configured runtime/model/tool provider. Retrieval derives chunks, embeddings, caches and memories with provenance; revocation invalidates reusable authority. Connectors receive provider-specific operations under owner grants. Email and push delivery receive necessary addressing/message payloads. Billing receives customer/subscription/usage identifiers; credentials remain encrypted in server-side stores. Audit records describe security actions without raw message bodies. Request telemetry uses bounded route/status metrics; aggregate product activity is opt-in and is not yet a validated customer-success measure.

Primary stores: PostgreSQL and private object/media storage in the selected deployment. Derivative/recipient stores: configured runtime/model providers, authorized connectors, email/push providers, customer devices/caches, backups and customer exports. Local Docker simulations do not establish a production subprocessors list or residency boundary. The regional policy's egress evidence is an administrative control; geographic placement and transfers require provider/deployment verification.

Retention is configurable by organization, with legal preservation taking precedence. Source deletion is a tracked process; backups, runtime/provider copies, derived data and downloaded exports can outlive a logical tombstone. Organization-wide server erasure now has a separate tracked workflow with legal preservation and external evidence gates. Global account erasure and complete independent-copy discovery/acceptance remain separate work. Do not promise immediate or universal deletion. State actual retention periods and independently verified deletion behavior in the reviewed notice.

Before publication, add user rights/request verification and appeal process; security and incident contact; applicable transfer safeguards; precise recording/device/analytics consent; minors policy; controller/processor responsibilities; disclosure/change notice process; current subprocessors and regions; retention schedule for operational/audit data; and legal exceptions. No production privacy notice has been published by this increment.
