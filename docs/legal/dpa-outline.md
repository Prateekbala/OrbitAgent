# Data processing agreement outline — unapproved

Complete named legal parties, agreement date, scope of services, subject matter, duration, processing purposes, categories of data subjects and personal data, controller instructions and applicable law. Cross-reference the approved service contract without implying that a local development instance has accepted these obligations.

The negotiated agreement should specify confidentiality and access authorization; technical/organizational measures; subprocessor authorization and objection/change process; assistance with verified rights requests and impact assessments; security incident notification and cooperation; international transfer mechanism and permitted locations; audit/evidence rights; return/deletion after termination with defined backup expiry and preservation exceptions; and allocation of responsibilities for customer-selected connectors and recording consent.

Attach actual measures and evidence: identity/tenant controls, tool/model/recording governance, restricted support, encryption/key custody, audit sink, vulnerability management, operational access review, backups/PITR/failover, deletion components/receipts, incident response and workforce controls. Distinguish implemented application controls from pending process/provider controls. Agree measurable commitments only after recovery and operational acceptance. Commercial liability, warranties and service levels require legal/commercial negotiation.

Open before signing: legal entity and contact; jurisdictions and transfers; production provider list; retention and deletion schedule; source/export limitations; independent assessment results; support staffing; incident deadlines; audit procedure; signature authority. This outline is not an executed DPA.
