# Privacy and enterprise contracting drafting pack

These documents are product/data-flow implementation inputs and unapproved contract outlines. They must not be presented as published legal terms or signed DPAs. The operator's legal entity, privacy contact, jurisdiction, subprocessors, deployment regions, transfer mechanism, retention commitments and commercial terms are not yet selected. Obtain qualified legal review before accepting customer data under a public privacy notice or DPA.

The [GDPR official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679) is a reference for review, including processor agreements and security obligations where applicable. Applicability and lawful bases depend on the actual controller, deployment and users; these templates make no compliance determination.

See [privacy and data flows](privacy-data-flows.md), [DPA outline](dpa-outline.md) and [subprocessor register](subprocessors.md). Maintain them against actual configured production services, independently of optional connector catalog availability.
