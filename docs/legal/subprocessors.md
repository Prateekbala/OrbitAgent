# Subprocessor register — configuration-dependent draft

No production provider has been approved or provisioned by this work. The connector catalog is not a list of services that automatically receive data.

Maintain a reviewed record for each actual provider: legal entity, service and purpose, data categories, regions and onward transfers, contractual/DPA references, security assessment date, deletion/retention behavior, encryption/key custody, incident contact, approval owner, change-notification date and exit/replacement plan.

Candidates requiring a record if selected include database/backup and infrastructure operators; private object/scanning/media services; runtime and model inference providers; email delivery; web/mobile push platform services; billing processor; secret management and telemetry operators; support tooling; and optional connected third-party systems to the extent the operator uses them as a processor. Customer-directed integrations need their own responsibility/authorization treatment.

Development alternatives currently include local PostgreSQL, MinIO, ClamAV, Mailpit, LiveKit and local model infrastructure. Local execution of these images does not approve their production vendors or establish a transfer mechanism. List a hosted service only once its actual legal entity, contract and deployment are verified. Publish the approved register and change process through a reviewed operator-controlled channel before enterprise launch.
